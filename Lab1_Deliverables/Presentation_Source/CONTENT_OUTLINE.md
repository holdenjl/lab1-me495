# Current poster and three-page handout outline

The central claim is a dimensioned first prototype: a 165 mm distal flywheel and
4.50 mm shaft reduce modeled peak gain about 44% and switching motion about 39%,
while preserving steady speed at the same voltage. Four physical alternatives
are presented with their limitations; no modified-hardware test is claimed.

## Poster

1. Problem, red apparatus-photo box, red dimensioned-CAD box, four-step workflow,
   data count and key baseline parameters.
2. Measured points and flexible-model curve only; frequency/peak comparison
   table; switching response of baseline and the preferred prototype.
3. Four response plots showing the shifted peaks, shaded extrapolation region,
   benefit table, physical build specifications and the main tradeoffs.

White background, navy section bars and a narrow gold recommendation strip follow
the exemplars. The red CAD box requests baseline/option-2 views plus a section
through the option-4 oil damper. No apparatus images are generated.

## Handout

1. Task, poster thumbnail, finding, recommendation and experimental approach.
2. Measured/model evidence, simple success criteria and selection reasoning.
3. Dimensioned hardware changes, mounting instructions, tradeoffs, next tests
   and source references. No differential equations or transfer-function algebra.

Twelve-point body type and short explanations support a reading time under ten
minutes. Full equations, sensitivity, conversions and checks are separately filed
in MATLAB. See EXEMPLAR_REVIEW.md in the delivery folder for the reference review.

## Checks

Baseline and asymmetric-wheel dynamics, finer transient sampling, inductance,
joint-compliance scenario, nominal targets, one-page poster, three-page handout,
no poster text overflow, and numerical/dimensional consistency across documents.
